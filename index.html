What I focus on

Automation that's engineered, not improvised.

A demo agent is easy; one you can trust with real work is not. The hard part is everything around the model: the right tools and context, guardrails, evaluation that catches regressions, monitoring, cost control and knowing when to bring a human in. That's the part I specialise in, whether the answer is an LLM agent, a classic ML model, or a simple script.

02 — Approach

How I take an agent from idea to production

The same loop I use at Canva and used with clients: small steps, real data, and metrics at every stage.

  1. 1

    Scope

    Map the workflow, find where time or quality is lost, and define what success means in hours, cost or accuracy.

  2. 2

    Prototype

    A working prototype on real data, with the agent's tools and context designed around the task.

  3. 3

    Evaluate

    An evaluation suite with clear metrics and test cases, so every change is measured and regressions are caught early.

  4. 4

    Deploy & monitor

    Integrated with existing tools, monitored in production, with fallbacks, cost control and human review where it matters.
